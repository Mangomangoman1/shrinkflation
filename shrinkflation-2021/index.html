What are examples of shrinkflation in 2021?

The biggest documented cuts in 2021 were: Mein Veggie Tag Veganer Bio Aufschnitt (140 g → 70 g), Denkmit Weichspüler Himmlische Frische (1.5 l → 1 l), Knorr Rahm Soße (102 g → 68 g), Nature Box Feste Duschpflege (150 g → 100 g), Beba Junior (8347 ml → 5797 ml). ShrinkRadar lists 49 sourced cases from 2021.

Which countries had the most shrinkflation cases in 2021?

In our library: Germany 35, United States 11, Canada 2, France 1. These counts follow where consumer agencies and reporters published lists, so they are not rates.

How much smaller did products get in 2021?

The median documented cut in 2021 was 13.3%, which is a 15.4% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Mein Veggie Tag Veganer Bio Aufschnitt, 140 g → 70 g (−50.0%).

Did prices per unit go up in 2021?

Where the source also recorded the shelf price before and after (35 of the 49 cases from 2021), 35 got more expensive per unit.
