Shrinkflation guides

Plain explanations of shrinkflation, built on our library of 691 documented cases and daily checks of 385 products.

  • Guide

    What is shrinkflation?

    Shrinkflation is when a pack gets smaller but the price stays the same or rises. Plain definition, how it differs from inflation and skimpflation, and 691 sourced examples.
  • Guide

    How to spot shrinkflation

    Practical checklist to catch shrinkflation: read the net weight, compare unit prices on shelf tags, check pack sizes, use the calculator, and search known cases.
