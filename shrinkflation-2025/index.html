FAQ

What are examples of shrinkflation in 2025?

The biggest documented cuts in 2025 were: Nesquik (500 g → 300 g), Ambi Pur luchtverfrisser (300 ml → 185 ml), Lindt Excellence Cacao Pur 82 % Cacao (80 g → 50 g), Wiltmann Bio Rein Rind Salami (80 g → 50 g), Knorr Veggie Wraps (370 g → 240 g). ShrinkRadar lists 85 sourced cases from 2025.

Which countries had the most shrinkflation cases in 2025?

In our library: Germany 28, Netherlands 20, United States 20, United Kingdom 7, Australia 5, Austria 3, Canada 2. These counts follow where consumer agencies and reporters published lists, so they are not rates.

How much smaller did products get in 2025?

The median documented cut in 2025 was 14.1%, which is a 16.4% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Nesquik, 500 g → 300 g (−40.0%).

Did prices per unit go up in 2025?

Where the source also recorded the shelf price before and after (48 of the 85 cases from 2025), 48 got more expensive per unit.
