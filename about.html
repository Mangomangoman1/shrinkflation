About

We check a fixed list of common products daily. Package size comes from Open Food Facts / Open Products Facts; prices come from the crowdsourced Open Prices database (most recent USD receipt/price tag). We only publish values we fetched; missing values are shown as missing.

A finding is logged when a newer snapshot shows a smaller net quantity, or a higher price per 100 g/ml.

Data: Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Prices (ODbL). Built 2026-10-13.
