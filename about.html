About ShrinkRadar

ShrinkRadar answers one question: did it shrink? We check a fixed list of everyday products daily. Net quantity comes from Open Food Facts / Open Products Facts; prices come from the crowdsourced Open Prices database (most recent US price). We only publish values we actually fetched — missing data is shown as missing, never estimated.

A finding is logged when a newer check shows a smaller net quantity in the same unit, or a higher price per 100 g/ml.

Known cases are historical before/after size cuts compiled from Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg’s Mogelpackungsliste, the Mouse Print* archive (Edgar Dworsky), Which?, CHOICE, CBC, foodwatch, Consumer Reports, LendingTree’s Wayback-Machine study, the U.S. Senate/USW report, news outlets and Wikipedia. Each case shows the source we fetched and a quote of what it says; cases we could not verify are left out.

Spotted a shrink? Corrections and tips are welcome.
