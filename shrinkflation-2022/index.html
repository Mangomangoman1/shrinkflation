How much smaller did products get in 2022?

The median documented cut in 2022 was 11.1%, which is a 12.5% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Wonnemeyer Rote Grütze (Aldi), 1000 g → 500 g (−50.0%).
