FAQ

What are examples of shrinkflation in 2023?

ShrinkRadar lists 134 sourced cases from 2023.

Which countries had the most shrinkflation cases in 2023?

In our library: Germany 71, United States 26, United Kingdom 23, Brazil 5, Canada 5, Austria 2, France 2. These counts follow where consumer agencies and reporters published lists, so they are not rates.

How much smaller did products get in 2023?

The median documented cut in 2023 was 13.3%, which is a 15.4% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Bohlsener Mühle Keksfreunde Schoko, 250 g → 125 g (−50.0%).

Did prices per unit go up in 2023?

Where the source also recorded the shelf price before and after (71 of the 134 cases from 2023), 71 got more expensive per unit.
