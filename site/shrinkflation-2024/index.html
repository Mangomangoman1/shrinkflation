The biggest documented cuts in 2024 were: Schwyzer Kräuterli Sanddorn-Orange (200 g → 75 g), Alverde Naturkosmetik Mineral Puder (10 g → 5 g), Biscotto Waffelblättchen mit Zartbitterschokolade (200 g → 100 g), Numis Med pH 5.5 Waschlotion (1000 ml → 500 ml), Lebensbaum Tomaten-Gewürzsalz (150 g → 80 g). ShrinkRadar lists 146 sourced cases from 2024.

Which countries had the most shrinkflation cases in 2024?

In our library: Germany 63, United States 37, Netherlands 18, Australia 14, Italy 9, United Kingdom 3, Canada 2. These counts follow where consumer agencies and reporters published lists, so they are not rates.

How much smaller did products get in 2024?

The median documented cut in 2024 was 12.5%, which is a 14.3% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Schwyzer Kräuterli Sanddorn-Orange, 200 g → 75 g (−62.5%).

Did prices per unit go up in 2024?

Where the source also recorded the shelf price before and after (81 of the 146 cases from 2024), 81 got more expensive per unit.
