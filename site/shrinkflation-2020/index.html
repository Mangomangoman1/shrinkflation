FAQ

What are examples of shrinkflation in 2020?

The biggest documented cuts in 2020 were: Mélange du Jardin Gewürzmischung (20 g → 10 g), Dein Bestes Katzenfutter-Senior (750 g → 450 g), Dr. Hauschka Gesichtsöl (30 ml → 18 ml), Rituals Dao Body Scrub (325 ml → 200 ml), Ebelin Nagellack Entferner Acetonfrei, Aloe Vera Duft (200 ml → 125 ml). ShrinkRadar lists 42 sourced cases from 2020.

Which countries had the most shrinkflation cases in 2020?

In our library: Germany 33, United States 8, Europe 1. These counts follow where consumer agencies and reporters published lists, so they are not rates.

How much smaller did products get in 2020?

The median documented cut in 2020 was 14.1%, which is a 16.5% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stayed the same. The biggest was Mélange du Jardin Gewürzmischung, 20 g → 10 g (−50.0%).

Did prices per unit go up in 2020?

Where the source also recorded the shelf price before and after (33 of the 42 cases from 2020), 33 got more expensive per unit.
