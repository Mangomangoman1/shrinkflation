Did Charmin Ultra Soft Super Mega Roll shrink?

Yes. It went from 396 sheets/roll to 366 sheets/roll, 7.6% less, around 2022. If the price stayed the same, that is a 8.2% hidden price increase per unit.

Source: Philadelphia Inquirer (Mar 15, 2022). source fetched 2026-10-13.
