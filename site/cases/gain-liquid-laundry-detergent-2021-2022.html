Did Gain liquid laundry detergent shrink?

Yes. It went from 165 fl oz to 154 fl oz, 6.7% less, around 2021–2022. If the price stayed the same, that is a 7.1% hidden price increase per unit.

Source: Philadelphia Inquirer (Mar 15, 2022). source fetched 2026-10-13.
