Shrinkflation in New Zealand

Has shrinkflation hit New Zealand? Yes. We have 10 documented cases from 2023–2026, each checked against the source that reported it. The median pack lost 8.8%; the biggest cut was Keri fruit juice bottle (New Zealand) (−20.0%). Main sources: watch.kepie.co.nz, consumer.org.nz, nzherald.co.nz.

FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit New Zealand?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 10 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in New Zealand from 2023–2026. The biggest: Keri fruit juice bottle (New Zealand), 3 l → 2.4 l (−20.0%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in New Zealand?

The most recently reported in our library: Graze Kinda-Like-A Dark Chocolate Berry Brownie (New Zealand) (400 g → 365 g, 2026), Cadbury Old Gold 70% dark chocolate block (New Zealand) (180 g → 165 g, 2026), Cadbury Favourites box, large (New Zealand, 2025) (520 g → 470 g, 2025).

Which brands shrank most often in New Zealand?

In our library: Cadbury (5), Graze (2). Big brands get more press coverage, so read this as “caught most often”, not a rate.

How much smaller do products get in New Zealand?

The median documented cut in New Zealand is 8.8%, a 9.7% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same. Where the source recorded prices before and after (4 cases), 4 got more expensive per unit.

Who reports shrinkflation in New Zealand?

Our New Zealand cases come from watch.kepie.co.nz, consumer.org.nz, nzherald.co.nz. Each case page quotes and links its source.
