Shrinkflation in the UK

Has shrinkflation hit the UK? Yes. We have 58 documented cases from 2015–2026, each checked against the source that reported it. The median pack lost 10.1%; the biggest cut was Sainsbury's Scottish Oats (−50.0%). Main sources: which.co.uk, theguardian.com, en.wikipedia.org, thegrocer.co.uk.

FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit the UK?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 58 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in the UK from 2015–2026. The biggest: Sainsbury's Scottish Oats, 1000 g → 500 g (−50.0%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in the UK?

The most recently reported in our library: Kenco Millicano instant coffee jar (Original/Americano and Decaf) (100 g → 90 g, 2026), Kenco Smooth / Rich / Decaf instant coffee jar, large (200 g → 190 g, 2026), Kenco Smooth / Rich / Decaf instant coffee jar, small (100 g → 95 g, 2026).

Which brands shrank most often in the UK?

In our library: Cadbury (9), Quality Street (4), Toblerone (4), Kenco (3), McVitie's (3), Terry's (3). Big brands get more press coverage, so read this as “caught most often”, not a rate.

How much smaller do products get in the UK?

The median documented cut in the UK is 10.1%, a 11.3% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same. Where the source recorded prices before and after (8 cases), 8 got more expensive per unit.

Who reports shrinkflation in the UK?

Our the UK cases come from which.co.uk, theguardian.com, en.wikipedia.org, thegrocer.co.uk, dev.to and 1 more sources. Each case page quotes and links its source.
