Did Cocoa Puffs Family Size shrink?

Yes. It went from 19.3 oz to 18.1 oz, 6.2% less, around 2021–2023. If the price stayed the same, that is a 6.6% hidden price increase per unit.

Source: United Steelworkers shrinkflation report (Mar 2024). source fetched 2026-10-13.
