Did Cottonelle Mega Roll (2-ply) shrink?

Yes. It went from 284 sheets/roll to 268 sheets/roll, 5.6% less, around 2022. If the price stayed the same, that is a 6.0% hidden price increase per unit.

Source: Philadelphia Inquirer (Mar 15, 2022). source fetched 2026-10-13.
