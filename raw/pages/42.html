Shrinkflation in New Zealand: packs that got smaller

Every pack kepie. Watch has reported getting smaller at PAK’nSAVE, New World or Woolworths, newest first. We print a change once we’ve read the new size more than once, or when two separate reports back it up and we’ve read the pack on the shelf ourselves.

Mon 5 to Sun 11 Oct 2026

Cadbury Old Gold 70% dark chocolate has shrunk from 180g to 165g. Same $7.00.

That's up 9% per 100g: $3.89 for the 180g, $4.24 for the 165g.

15g less in the pack for the same money. Both sizes are at Woolworths right now, so grab the 180g while you can.

Old pack (180g)$3.89 per 100g
New pack (165g)$4.24 per 100g
Shelf price$7.00 for both

How we know: We've checked both at Woolworths since the 165g turned up on 15 June, and they've cost the same every time, specials included. We checked them over the past 8 weeks and both were on special in 4 of them.

Mon 21 to Sun 27 Sept 2026

Cadbury Favourites went from 570g to 520g, and now 470g.

18% less in the pack since May 2023.

Over the last eight weeks the 470g pack has cost from $9.49 at PAK'nSAVE to $20.00 at Woolworths, or $2.02 to $4.26 per 100g.

PAK'nSAVE, 470g$9.49 to $14.99 in the last eight weeks
Woolworths, 470g$9.99 to $20.00 in the last eight weeks
New World, 470g$9.99 to $15.99 in the last eight weeks

How we know: We didn't see these size changes ourselves. Our price history starts in April 2026 and every change here happened before that, so each one comes from at least two published reports, linked below. What we did measure is the pack on the shelf now: 470g at all three supermarkets.

Where this comes from: NZ Herald (570g to 520g, May 2023) · 1News (570g to 520g, May 2023) · Price Check Guy (TikTok) (520g to 470g, by December 2025) · Cadbury's own 470g listing (520g to 470g, by December 2025)
