# ShrinkRadar

**Did it shrink?** [ShrinkRadar](https://shrinkradar.com/) tracks shrinkflation in groceries and household goods: products that get smaller while the price stays the same or goes up.

- **Daily checks** of the net quantity and latest price of everyday products, from Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Prices.
- **A library of documented cases**: before/after package-size cuts reported by consumer agencies (Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg, Consumentenbond, Which?, CHOICE), consumer advocates (Mouse Print*) and news investigations. Every case links the source it came from. We leave out cases we can't verify, and we never estimate missing values.

## Open data

The whole case library is free to download and reuse under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- CSV: https://shrinkradar.com/data/cases.csv
- JSON: https://shrinkradar.com/data/cases.json
- Field list, coverage notes and how to cite: https://shrinkradar.com/data/

Please credit **ShrinkRadar (shrinkradar.com)** and link the case page or the original source when you quote a figure. The files leave out the source quotes, which belong to their publishers. Daily product snapshots come from Open Food Facts / Open Products Facts / Open Prices and stay under their [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/) licence.

## Report a shrink

Spotted a smaller pack? Use the [Submit a sighting](https://shrinkradar.com/submit/) form, which opens a prefilled issue here, or [open an issue](https://github.com/Mangomangoman1/shrinkflation/issues) directly. Please include the old and new net quantity and a source or photo. We verify every report before adding it.

## How it's built

A static site generated by `build_site.py` (Python standard library, plus Pillow for the share image) into `site/`. It is served from the `gh-pages` branch by GitHub Pages. `check.py` runs the daily product checks, `merge_cases.py` merges the case sources in `raw/` into `data/cases.json`, `validate_structured_data.py` and `check_links.py` are pre-publish checks, and `publish.sh` builds, pushes and pings IndexNow.
