Shrinkflation in the Netherlands

Has shrinkflation hit the Netherlands? Yes. We have 56 documented cases from 2024–2026, each checked against the source that reported it. The median pack lost 13.4%; the biggest cut was Nesquik (−40.0%). Main sources: consumentenbond.nl.

FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit the Netherlands?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 56 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in the Netherlands from 2024–2026. The biggest: Nesquik, 500 g → 300 g (−40.0%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in the Netherlands?

The most recently reported in our library: Dettol Original Allesreiniger (1500 ml → 1000 ml, 2026), Peijnenburg Gemberkoek (465 g → 348 g, 2026), Jumbo Macadamiamix Gezouten (130 g → 100 g, 2026).

Which brands shrank most often in the Netherlands?

In our library: AH (4), Jumbo (3), Andrélon (2), Danerolles (2), Peijnenburg (2). Big brands get more press coverage, so read this as “caught most often”, not a rate.

How much smaller do products get in the Netherlands?

The median documented cut in the Netherlands is 13.4%, a 15.5% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same. Where the source recorded prices before and after (56 cases), 56 got more expensive per unit.

Who reports shrinkflation in the Netherlands?

Our the Netherlands cases come from consumentenbond.nl. Each case page quotes and links its source.
