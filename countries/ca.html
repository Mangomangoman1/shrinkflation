Shrinkflation in Canada

Has shrinkflation hit Canada? Yes. We have 11 documented cases from 2021–2025, each checked against the source that reported it. The median pack lost 10.0%; the biggest cut was Redpath sugar (Walmart test) (−25.0%). Main sources: cbc.ca.

FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit Canada?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 11 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in Canada from 2021–2025. The biggest: Redpath sugar (Walmart test), 2000 g → 1500 g (−25.0%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in Canada?

The most recently reported in our library: Mars fun size Halloween candy (65 mini bars) (782 g → 672 g, 2025), Hershey's Halloween 50 mini candy bars (513 g → 481 g, 2025), Redpath sugar (Walmart test) (2000 g → 1500 g, 2024).

How much smaller do products get in Canada?

The median documented cut in Canada is 10.0%, a 11.1% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same.

Who reports shrinkflation in Canada?

Our Canada cases come from cbc.ca. Each case page quotes and links its source.
