Shrinkflation in Italy

Has shrinkflation hit Italy? Yes. We have 13 documented cases from 2022–2024, each checked against the source that reported it. The median pack lost 7.1%; the biggest cut was Vidal muschio bianco body wash (large) (−13.3%). Main sources: altroconsumo.it, finanza.lastampa.it.

FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit Italy?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 13 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in Italy from 2022–2024. The biggest: Vidal muschio bianco body wash (large), 750 ml → 650 ml (−13.3%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in Italy?

The most recently reported in our library: Vidal muschio bianco body wash (large) (750 ml → 650 ml, 2024), Beck's can (Italy) (50 cl → 44 cl, 2024), Nidra soap bar (100 g → 90 g, 2024).

Which brands shrank most often in Italy?

In our library: Rigoni di Asiago (3). Big brands get more press coverage, so read this as “caught most often”, not a rate.

How much smaller do products get in Italy?

The median documented cut in Italy is 7.1%, a 7.7% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same.

Who reports shrinkflation in Italy?

Our Italy cases come from altroconsumo.it, finanza.lastampa.it. Each case page quotes and links its source.
