FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit France?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 5 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in France from 2019–2023. The biggest: Saint Louis white sugar, 750 g → 650 g (−13.3%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in France?

The most recently reported in our library: Auchan jambon supérieur slices (45 g → 40 g, 2023), Carrefour Reflets de France Corsican orange marmalade (325 g → 315 g, 2023), St Hubert Omega 3 margarine (240 g → 230 g, 2022).

How much smaller do products get in France?

The median documented cut in France is 10.0%, a 11.1% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same.
