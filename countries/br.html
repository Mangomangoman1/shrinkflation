FAQ

Has shrinkflation hit Brazil?

Yes. ShrinkRadar has 5 documented, sourced shrinkflation cases in Brazil from 2023. The biggest: Piraquê wafer, 160 g → 100 g (−37.5%).

What are recent examples of shrinkflation in Brazil?

The most recently reported in our library: Piraquê wafer (160 g → 100 g, 2023), Tang powdered drink (25 g → 18 g, 2023), Piraquê Maizena biscuits (200 g → 175 g, 2023).

Which brands shrank most often in Brazil?

In our library: Piraquê (2). Big brands get more press coverage, so read this as “caught most often”, not a rate.

How much smaller do products get in Brazil?

The median documented cut in Brazil is 12.5%, a 14.3% hidden price rise per unit if the shelf price stays the same.

Who reports shrinkflation in Brazil?

Our Brazil cases come from procon.niteroi.rj.gov.br. Each case page quotes and links its source.
